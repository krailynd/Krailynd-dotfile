# Hackathon MINEDU Competitive Intelligence - 2022-2026

## Overview
This reference document captures competitive intelligence and historical analysis of the Ministerio de Educación (MINEDU) Hackathon events from 2022 to 2026, specifically focused on the "Hackathon en Tecnologías Digitales" and "Hackathon en Robótica Educativa" competitions. This data is critical for Krailynd's participation in the 2026 edition.

---

## Historical Event Analysis

### 2022: First Edition (Robótica Educativa)
- **Focus**: Proponer soluciones tecnológicas mediante diseño, construcción y programación de robots
- **Objective**: Optimizar el uso de la robótica educativa en el proceso educativo
- **Categories**: Expertos, Docentes, Estudiantes
- **Source**: [Slideshare - Bases 2022](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/baseshackathon2022pdf/254969221)

### 2023: Second Edition (Robótica Educativa)
- **Participants**: 97 estudiantes from Lima and Callao + docentes + expertos
- **Winning Project (Category C)**: Mecanismo automatizado de superficies flotantes para recolección de basura en el mar
- **Technology Stack**: Sensores de proximidad, motores, automatización, materiales resistentes al agua
- **Team**: Selene Córdova, Eduardo Vergaray (Club de Robótica "Steam DIY", mentor: Martín Cabada)
- **Impact**: Contaminación marina (ODS 14: Vida submarina)
- **Awards**: Tablets and Chromebooks (donated by AVIC International Beijing and Nivel A Educación SAC)
- **Source**: [Gob.pe 2023](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minedu/noticias/702476-minedu-premia-a-estudiantes-ganadores-de-hackathon-en-robotica-educativa)

### 2024: Bicentenario Edition
- **Participants**: 132 estudiantes from 18 regions + 46 docentes + 24 expertos
- **Location**: Huampaní, Chosica (Lima)
- **Dates**: August 2-4, 2024
- **Focus**: Robótica Educativa + Tecnologías Digitales
- **Winning Themes**: 
  - Robots para personas con discapacidad
  - Sistemas de alerta temprana (inundaciones, deslizamientos)
  - Automatización de riego en cultivos
- **Awards**: Tabletas, kits de programación Arduino y ZMROBO
- **Innovation**: Soluciones ganadoras publicadas en plataforma educativa PerúEduca
- **Sources**: [Andina](https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-gran-hackathon-2024-escolares-docentes-y-expertos-presentaran-sus-proyectos-994005.aspx), [La República](https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2024/08/04/termino-la-hackathon-2024-estudiantes-peruanos-crearon-robots-para-ayudar-a-personas-con-discapacidad-minedu-tecnologia-morgan-quero-399008)

### 2025: Fourth Edition
- **Participants**: 204 total (Expertos: 36 | Docentes: 44 | Estudiantes: 124)
- **Focus**: Inteligencia Artificial + Robótica + STEAM
- **Trends**: Female participation increased 28% between 2023 and 2025
- **Winning Teams (Category A)**:
  - 🥇 TargAiSquad (high-impact AI proposal)
  - 🥈 AILearn (adaptive learning system)
- **Dominant Themes**: IA aplicada a la educación, Robótica para inclusión
- **Special Project**: Inti Ñawina (mochila inteligente con visión artificial para reducir riesgo de feminicidio)
- **Awards**: Laptops, kits tecnológicos
- **Sources**: [Gob.pe](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/minedu/noticias/1220955-minedu-premia-el-talento-estudiantil-y-docente-por-innovar-con-inteligencia-artificial-y-robotica-en-hackathon-2025), [Proactivo](https://proactivo.com.pe/minedu-premia-el-talento-estudiantil-y-docente-en-hackathon-2025/)

## 2026 Edition: Current Bases Analysis

### Categories and Target Audiences

#### Category A: Expertos en Tecnologías Digitales
- **Profile**: Especialistas con experiencia en innovación tecnológica aplicada a la educación
- **Objective**: Diseñar estrategias innovadoras de alto nivel técnico
- **2026 Challenges**:
  1. Propuestas creativas, viables y escalables para promover acceso y uso eficiente de tecnologías digitales en aulas
  2. Integrar tecnologías emergentes para la atención de estudiantes de Educación Básica Especial

#### Category B: Docentes de Educación Básica
- **Profile**: Docentes con experiencia en recursos digitales
- **Objective**: Proponer estrategias pedagógicas innovadoras
- **2026 Challenges**:
  1. Desarrollar estrategias que optimicen la integración y aprovechamiento de tecnologías digitales en enseñanza-aprendizaje
  2. Mejorar metodologías educativas para fortalecer competencias digitales

#### Category C: Estudiantes (3ro-5to Secundaria)
- **Profile**: Estudiantes del VII ciclo de Educación Básica Regular
- **Objective**: Crear, diseñar y programar soluciones tecnológicas
- **Team Composition**: 1 docente/tutor + 3 estudiantes
- **2026 Challenges**:
  1. **Desafío 1**: Sistema de alerta temprana comunitaria (SATC) ante deslizamientos, huaycos, inundaciones o aguas estancadas
  2. **Desafío 2**: Sistema para uso eficiente del agua durante el regadío de cultivos
  3. **Desafío 3**: Sistema para ventilación natural al amanecer O alertas para adherencia a tratamiento contra TB
  4. **Desafío 4**: Innovación Local (resolver problemática específica de la comunidad/región)

- **Communication**: Bluetooth (HC-05), WiFi (ESP32), LoRa (long distance)

## Winning Project Patterns

### Successful Project Characteristics
1. **Real-World Impact**: Projects that solve actual community problems
2. **Scalability**: Solutions that can be implemented across multiple regions
3. **Cost-Effectiveness**: Use of affordable, locally available materials
4. **Technical Innovation**: Creative use of sensors and automation
5. **Educational Value**: Projects that can be integrated into school curricula
6. **Sustainability**: Solutions aligned with SDG (ODS) goals

### Common Success Factors
- **Maqueta Size**: 35x35 cm (standard for physical prototypes)
- **Documentation**: Complete with circuit diagrams, code, and user manuals
- **Presentation**: Clear demonstration of functionality and impact
- **Team Collaboration**: Effective division of roles (programming, mechanics, documentation)

### Judging Criteria (Inferred)
1. **Innovation** (30%): Novelty and creativity of the solution
2. **Technical Implementation** (25%): Quality of hardware/software implementation
3. **Impact** (20%): Potential benefit to the community/education
4. **Presentation** (15%): Clarity and effectiveness of demonstration
5. **Documentation** (10%): Completeness and quality of supporting materials

## Competitive Intelligence: Ica Region Specific

### Regional Context
- **Climate**: Arid, high UV radiation levels, water scarcity
- **Economy**: Agriculture (vineyards, cotton), tourism
- **Education**: Multiple schools with limited shade in patios
- **Health**: High skin cancer rates due to UV exposure
- **Agriculture**: Heavy water usage, need for efficient irrigation

### Problem Opportunities for Ica
1. **UV Radiation**: High levels throughout the year, especially affecting school children
2. **Water Scarcity**: Critical issue for agriculture, need for efficient irrigation
3. **Disaster Preparedness**: Risk of earthquakes, need for early warning systems
4. **Agricultural Efficiency**: Need for precision agriculture technologies
5. **Educational Technology**: Limited access to digital tools in rural schools

### Recommended Focus Areas
1. **UV Protection**: SafeSchool-UV project
2. **Water Conservation**: AquaSmart Ica project
3. **Disaster Alerts**: Seismic early warning systems
4. **Agricultural Monitoring**: Soil moisture and weather monitoring

## Success Strategy for 2026

### 1. Project Selection
- **Choose**: SafeSchool-UV or AquaSmart Ica
- **Rationale**: Both have strong historical precedent and regional relevance
- **Differentiation**: Focus on Ica-specific implementation and measurable impact

### 2. Team Composition
- **Roles**:
  - **Programmer**: Arduino/ESP32 coding, sensor integration
  - **Mechanical Designer**: Physical structure, motor systems
  - **Documentation Specialist**: Manuals, diagrams, presentation
  - **Tutor**: Supervision, educational alignment

### 3. Development Timeline
- **Week 1-2**: Research and design
- **Week 3**: Component procurement
- **Week 4-5**: Prototype development
- **Week 6**: Testing and refinement
- **Week 7**: Documentation and presentation preparation

### 4. Documentation Requirements
- **Technical**:
  - Circuit diagrams (Fritzing)
  - Source code (commented)
  - Bill of materials
- **User**:
  - Assembly instructions
  - Operating manual
  - Maintenance guide
- **Presentation**:
  - Problem statement
  - Solution description
  - Impact analysis
  - Demonstration video (1-2 minutes)

### 5. Presentation Strategy
- **Structure**:
  1. Problem Identification (30 seconds)
  2. Solution Overview (1 minute)
  3. Technical Implementation (1 minute)
  4. Demonstration (1 minute)
  5. Impact and Scalability (30 seconds)
- **Visuals**:
  - Physical prototype
  - Circuit diagram
  - Block diagram
  - Cost analysis
- **Key Messages**:
  - Real-world problem
  - Innovative solution
  - Measurable impact
  - Scalable implementation
