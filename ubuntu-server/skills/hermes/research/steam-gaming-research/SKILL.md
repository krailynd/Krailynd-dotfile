### 3. **Game Research Workflow**

#### Step 1: Identify Target Criteria
- **Budget:** Maximum price in PEN or USD
- **Genre:** Open-world, RPG, action, etc.
- **Mod Support:** Nexus Mods, Steam Workshop
- **YouTube Appeal:** Visual quality, gameplay variety, mod potential

#### Step 2: Search Steam Database
**Primary Sources:**
- [Steam Store (Peru)](https://store.steampowered.com/?l=spanish&cc=PE)
- [SteamDB Sales](https://steamdb.info/sales/)

**Search Filters:**
- Price range: Under $10 USD or equivalent in PEN
- Discount percentage: 50% or higher
- User reviews: 5,000+ reviews, 80%+ positive
- Tags: "Open World", "Mods", "RPG", "Action"

#### Step 3: Verify Mod Support
**Modding Communities:**
- [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/)
- [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/)
- [Mod DB](https://www.moddb.com/)

**Mod Support Tiers:**
- ✅ **Tier 1:** Thousands of mods (Skyrim, Fallout 4, GTA V)
- ✅ **Tier 2:** Hundreds of mods (Just Cause 3, Sleeping Dogs)
- ⚠️ **Tier 3:** Limited mods (Mad Max, Mafia III)
- ❌ **Tier 4:** No mods (Most AAA games without modding tools)

## 🔧 Tools and Commands

### Steam Price Check
```bash
# Check current price in Peru (PEN)
curl -s "https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=<APP_ID>&cc=pe&l=spanish" | jq

# Example: Skyrim Special Edition (APP_ID: 489830)
curl -s "https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=489830&cc=pe&l=spanish" | jq '.489830.data.price_overview.final'
```

### Currency Conversion
```bash
# Convert USD to PEN (using exchange rate 1 USD = 3.70 PEN)
echo "scale=2; $USD_PRICE * 3.70" | bc
```

## 📌 Pitfalls and Gotchas

1. **Regional Pricing Differences:**
   - Prices in Peru (PEN) may differ from USD prices due to regional adjustments.
   - Always check the **Steam store with `cc=pe`** for accurate pricing.

2. **Sale Duration:**
   - Steam sales typically last **2 weeks** (e.g., Summer Sale: June 25 - July 9).
   - **Last-minute deals** may appear in the final 48 hours.

3. **Mod Compatibility:**
   - Some mods may **break** after game updates.
   - Always check **mod comments** for compatibility with the latest version.

4. **Performance Impact:**
   - Graphics mods (e.g., ENB for Skyrim) can **significantly reduce FPS**.
   - Test mods on **low settings first** before recording.

5. **Copyright Issues:**
   - Using **copyrighted music** in YouTube videos can lead to **strikes or demonetization**.
   - Stick to **royalty-free music** or in-game audio.

## 🎯 Recommendations for Content Creators

### For New YouTubers (Budget: 40-50 PEN)
1. **Start with Just Cause 3 + Sleeping Dogs** (40 PEN total).
   - High visual appeal, easy to create engaging content.
2. **Focus on short, high-energy videos** (5-10 minutes).
3. **Use free editing software** (CapCut, Shotcut, OBS for recording).

### For Mod-focused Channels
1. **Prioritize Skyrim or Fallout 4** (even if it means stretching the budget).
2. **Showcase mods in categories**:
   - Graphics mods
   - Gameplay mods
   - New quests/mods
3. **Collaborate with mod authors** for exclusive content.

### For Variety Channels
1. **Buy 2-3 games** within budget (e.g., Mad Max + Saint's Row IV + Enslaved).
2. **Create themed playlists** (e.g., "Open World Adventures", "Post-Apocalyptic Games").
3. **Mix gameplay with commentary** to add personality.
