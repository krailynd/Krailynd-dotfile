## 🔍 Alternative Platforms (If Steam Prices Are Too High)

### 1. **Epic Games Store**
- **Free Games:** Weekly free titles (e.g., GTA V was free in 2020).
- **Exclusive Deals:** Sometimes better than Steam.
- **Downside:** No mod support for most games.

### 2. **GOG (Good Old Games)**
- **DRM-Free:** No DRM restrictions.
- **Older Titles:** Classic games with mod support.
- **Downside:** Smaller library, fewer sales.

### 3. **Fanatical / Humble Bundle**
- **Bundles:** Multiple games for a single price.
- **Downside:** Limited selection, not always open-world.

### 4. **Key Resellers (Use with Caution)**
- **G2A, Kinguin, Eneba:** Often cheaper than Steam.
- **Risk:** Potential for scams or invalid keys.
- **Recommendation:** Only buy from **trusted sellers** with high ratings.
